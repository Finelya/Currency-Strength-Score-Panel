Extract the downloaded Zip file, then copy the .ex5 file (green).
After that,
1. Open your MT5.
2. Click the File Menu -> Open Data Folder.
3. Click the MQL5 Folder -> Select the Expert Folder.
4. Paste the .ex5 file into the Expert folder.
5. Restart (close and then reopen) your MT5.
6. In the Navigator (under your login account name), find the Expert Advisor (under the "Indicators" heading).
7. Select the .ex5 file name you just pasted.
8. Drag it to the desired chart; uncheck "auto trade."
9. Adjust the zone to your liking, then click "OK."
