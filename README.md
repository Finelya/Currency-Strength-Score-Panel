# Currency-Strength-Score-Panel
Multi-Currency Momentum &amp; Trend Dashboard Stop guessing which currency pairs to trade. The Strength Score Panel is an all-in-one visual dashboard designed to help you instantly identify the strongest and weakest currencies across the market in real time.
By scanning all 28 major and cross currency pairs simultaneously, this indicator calculates a precise strength score for the 8 major currencies (USD, EUR, JPY, NZD, CAD, GBP, CHF, AUD), allowing you to pair the strongest currency against the weakest for high-probability setups.

Key Features

• 28-Pair Real-Time Scanner: Automatically analyzes all 28 major currency pairs to deliver accurate, live strength rankings on a clean 0–100 scale.
• Weighted Scoring Logic: Uses a custom multi-factor weighting formula (40 / 30 / 20 / 10) to filter out market noise and highlight true momentum shifts.
• Color-Coded Visual Bars: Instantly spot bullish leaders (green), neutral currencies (grey), and bearish laggards (red) at a glance.
• Built-In Candle Countdown Timer: Displays the exact time remaining until the current candle closes so you can time your entries with precision.
• Clean & Lightweight UI: Features a modern, dark-themed dashboard with a manual Refresh button and last-update timestamp that won't clutter your chart or slow down your terminal.

How to Use It

1. Find the Divergence: Look at the top of the panel (#1 and #2) for the strongest currencies and the bottom (#7 and #8) for the weakest.
2. Select the Best Pair: Match the strongest currency with the weakest (e.g., if USD is #1 with a score of 85.4 and AUD is #8 with 24.9, look for short setups on AUDUSD).
3. Confirm with Price Action: Combine the currency strength bias with your favorite trendlines, moving averages, or Bollinger Bands to execute high-confidence trades.

Specifications

• Platform: MetaTrader 5

• Pairs Monitored: All 28 Major & Cross Pairs

• Timeframes: Works on all timeframes (M1 to MN)
